A Message From Sharon

First of all, thank you for taking the time to visit my page. Whether you choose to donate, share my story, or simply keep me in your prayers, I truly appreciate your kindness.

My life changed dramatically in April 2026 when I suffered a stroke. Recovery has been slow, challenging, and filled with many medical appointments, hospital visits, specialist consultations, tests, therapy, and medications.

Alongside recovering from the stroke, I continue living with rheumatoid arthritis, asthma, vertigo, vision problems, lower-back pain, spondylitis, and recurring swelling in my feet.

For several years, I have also needed spinal surgery, but because of financial difficulties, I have been unable to move forward with it. The estimated cost has continued to rise, making it even more difficult to afford.

Every contribution, regardless of size, helps me move one step closer toward healing, recovery, and a healthier future.
